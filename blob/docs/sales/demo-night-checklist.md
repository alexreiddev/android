# Night demo checklist

**Pack**
- [ ] 5–8 working units, fully charged, tested the night before
- [ ] Stakes, spare cells, mode magnet
- [ ] Printed one-page brief ×3 ([site]/resorts/brief → Print)
- [ ] Phone or camera that shoots well in the dark, small tripod
- [ ] Notebook; pilot terms draft

**On site (arrive before dusk)**
- [ ] Walk the path with the owner in daylight; agree where lights go
- [ ] Place lights 3–5 m apart on one stretch, at turns and steps first
- [ ] Wait for full dark. Let the owner walk it alone first, then with you
- [ ] Ask a staff member to walk it and swap one cell
- [ ] Film: one wide shot of the path waking, one close-up of a single light. Ask before filming people

**Ask**
- [ ] Too bright, too dim, or right?
- [ ] Does it stay on long enough?
- [ ] Where else would you want these?
- [ ] Who would look after them?

**Before you leave**
- [ ] Offer to leave the set overnight
- [ ] Agree the next step and a date (pilot terms, quote, or no)

**Next morning**
- [ ] Send photos and a thank-you (message 4 in `outreach-messages.md`)
- [ ] Update the enquiry `status` and your `shortlist.csv`
- [ ] Write down every complaint about the light. That list is your product roadmap.
