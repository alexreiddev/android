# Shortlist: which properties to approach first

Score each property 0–2 on every line. Approach the highest scores first. Aim for 50 on the list, 10 in the first wave.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paths in the dark** | Single building, lit corridors | Some outdoor walking | Cottages, tents or villas spread through garden, plantation or forest |
| **Canopy / monsoon** | Open lawns, full sun | Mixed | Heavy tree cover, hill or rainforest setting |
| **Cares about the night** | Bright, urban style | Neutral | Sells nature, wildlife, stargazing, quiet |
| **Reachable owner** | Chain, head office elsewhere | Manager on site | Owner-run, owner on site |
| **Distance from you** | Over 4 hours | 2–4 hours | Under 2 hours (you can demo and service it) |
| **Photogenic** | Hard to film | Fine | Would make a striking night film |
| **Size** | Under 10 lights needed | 10–50 | 50+ |

**Total out of 14.** 10+ = first wave. 7–9 = second wave. Below 7 = later.

**Skip for now:** large chains (slow purchasing), properties needing security lighting (wrong product), anywhere you can't reach to service a pilot.

**Where to find them:** your own network first, then homestay and plantation-stay listings by district (Wayanad, Idukki/Munnar, Thekkady, Alappuzha backwaters, Kannur, Thrissur hills), district tourism councils, and architects or landscape designers who work on resorts.

Track them in `shortlist.csv`.
