# Outreach messages

Rules: one property at a time, by name. Short. Never claim tested specs, ratings or customers we don't have. Replace everything in [brackets].

## 1. First WhatsApp (owner or manager)

> Hello [Name], I'm [Your name] from BLOB, a small lighting studio in Kerala.
> We're making path lights for stays like [Property]: small warm lights that stay dark until a guest walks by, then fade. No wiring, no solar.
> We're choosing 3 Kerala properties for our first pilot. Could I show you a few working units one evening? It takes 20 minutes.
> One-page brief: [site]/resorts/brief

## 2. First email

**Subject:** Path lights for [Property] that only wake for guests

> Hello [Name],
>
> I'm [Your name], founder of BLOB. We design small, warm path lights in Kerala. They sleep in the dark, wake gently when someone walks by, and fade after they pass. There's no wiring or trenching, and they don't depend on solar, so they work under trees and through the monsoon.
>
> I'm writing because [one specific, true reason: your cottage paths run through the plantation / guests mention the walk back at night / you keep the property dark for wildlife].
>
> We're pre-launch and choosing 3 Kerala properties for a pilot: discounted units in exchange for honest feedback and permission to photograph the path at night.
>
> Could I bring a few units over one evening and show you? A one-page brief is here: [site]/resorts/brief
>
> Warmly,
> [Your name] · [phone]

## 3. Follow-up (5–7 days later, once)

> Hello [Name], just following up on the path lights. If evenings are busy, I can leave a demo set at reception for a night and collect it the next day. No obligation. Would [day] suit?

## 4. After a demo

> Thank you for last night, [Name]. Here are the photos from your path: [link].
> If you'd like to go ahead with the pilot, I'd suggest [N] lights on [the path we walked]. I'll send the pilot terms today. Anything you'd want changed about the light itself: brighter, dimmer, longer?

## 5. Asking for a quote (after the first month of a pilot)

> [Name], now that the lights have been in for a month, would you be willing to share a sentence or two about them that we can use with your name and [Property]? Only if you're happy with them, and I'll send the exact wording for your approval first.

## Visit / call script (5 questions)

1. How do guests get back to their rooms at night today?
2. What have you tried for path lighting, and what went wrong? (wiring cost, solar under trees, glare, theft)
3. How many metres of path, and how many steps or turns?
4. Who would look after the lights day to day?
5. If these worked well on one path, what would you want next?

Write the answers down in the enquiry's `message` or your notes the same day.
